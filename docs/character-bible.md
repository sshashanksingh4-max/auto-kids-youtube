# Hindi Kids Universe — Character Bible

## Core cast

### Chintu — चिंटू
Curious 7-year-old Indian boy. Short black hair, yellow hoodie, blue shorts, red sneakers. Personality: curious, brave, kind, playful.

### Mini — मिन्नी
Clever 7-year-old Indian girl. Black hair in two ponytails with colorful clips, turquoise top, purple skirt, white sneakers. Personality: clever, calm, creative, helpful.

### Golu — गोलू
Cute brown squirrel with cream belly, large expressive eyes, tiny green backpack. Personality: funny, energetic, kind, mischievous.

### Tinku — टिंकू
Friendly compact helper robot. Rounded white and sky-blue body, friendly glowing eyes, small antenna, orange accent lights. Personality: helpful, curious, logical, gentle.

## Visual consistency rules
- Bright polished 3D cartoon appearance.
- Family-friendly expressions and actions.
- Keep clothing, colors, proportions, and species consistent across episodes.
- Avoid horror, gore, weapons, adult themes, or realistic danger.
- Use simple readable compositions that work for children and mobile screens.

## Story rules
Every episode should have a distinct premise, escalating discovery, a safe problem to solve, character interaction, and a positive lesson. Recurring characters can return, but the plot, setting, challenge, and dialogue must vary meaningfully.
